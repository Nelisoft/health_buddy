Our Story

Healthcare, the way it
should feel.

HealthBuddy was built on a simple conviction — that trusted health products and expert advice should be accessible, personal, and honest. We're a small team from Lagos, working to make that real.

Where We Began

A quiet frustration
turned into a promise.

In 2024, our founder spent weeks trying to find a reliable supplement and a doctor willing to answer basic questions. She found neither — until she pieced together answers from strangers on the internet.

That experience became HealthBuddy. A place where every product is verified, every practitioner is licensed, and every conversation starts with listening.

We believe good health isn't a luxury. It's a foundation.
